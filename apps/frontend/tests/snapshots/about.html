Our Story

From a single CNC shop to a global education movement — TITANS of Manufacturing is on a mission to save manufacturing education and inspire the next generation of makers.

Founded in 2012 by Titan Gilroy, TITANS of CNC began as a precision machine shop in California and grew into the world's largest manufacturing education platform. Our 100% free, project-based courses have empowered hundreds of thousands of students worldwide to start or advance their careers in CNC machining, aerospace manufacturing, grinding, and Swiss machining.
